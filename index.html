08Frequently asked questions

Good answers before you enquire.

Know what to send, what can be confirmed and what happens after you request a quote.

Can you fit glass at my home or workplace?

Mobile enquiries are welcome across the East Rand. Share your town or suburb so the team can confirm availability for your location and vehicle.

Can a chip be repaired instead of replacing the windscreen?

That depends on the damage, its position and the vehicle. Describe the damage and share a clear photo in WhatsApp so the team can advise on the next assessment step.

What details help you quote the correct glass?

Send the vehicle make, model, year if known, the glass panel involved and your town or suburb. You can attach damage photos in WhatsApp.

Do you accept fleet and corporate enquiries?

Yes. Include your business name, vehicle types, fleet size, site locations and any quotation or invoicing requirements.

Does a website enquiry confirm a booking or final price?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Glass compatibility, availability, pricing and booking details are confirmed directly by the business.

Can I send photos with my quote request?

The website does not upload photos. Once the prefilled message opens in WhatsApp, attach clear photos of the damaged glass and any useful vehicle details before sending.

Help & information

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about vehicle details, mobile enquiries, glass identification and what happens after you request a quote.

Do I need to know my vehicle’s year?

The year helps identify the correct glass, but if you are unsure, leave it blank and provide the make, model and any other details you know. The team can follow up to identify the right option.

Which areas do you cover?

The website currently lists mobile auto-glass enquiries across the East Rand, Gauteng. Share your town or suburb so the team can confirm availability for your vehicle and location.

Can you come to my home or workplace?

Mobile fitting enquiries are welcome. The team must confirm whether your vehicle, glass type and location can be accommodated.

Can a chip be repaired instead of replacing the windscreen?

That depends on the damage, its size and position, and the vehicle. Share a clear photo and vehicle details for an assessment.

What details help identify the correct glass?

Provide the make, model, year if known, the glass panel involved, your location and any relevant sensors, camera brackets, antenna or trim details.

What if I do not know the exact glass type?

Choose the option to help identify the glass and describe the vehicle. You can attach photos in WhatsApp after the prefilled enquiry opens.

Can I send photos through the website?

The current website does not upload photo files. Open the prefilled WhatsApp message, attach clear photos of the damage and vehicle if useful, then press Send.

Do you work with fleets and businesses?

Fleet and corporate enquiries are welcome. Include the business name, fleet size, vehicle types, site locations and any quote or invoicing requirements you want the team to review.

Can you help with insurance-related enquiries?

You can describe your insurer and claim situation. The process and outcome depend on the insurer and policy, so the team must confirm the next steps for your case.

How much does replacement glass cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, glass specification, availability, location and fitting requirements. Request a quote; the website does not display unverified prices.

Does my enquiry confirm stock or a booking?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Compatibility, availability, final price and booking details must be confirmed directly.

How do I contact Infinity?

Call 084 526 2956, email infinityautoglass@outlook.com or use the WhatsApp links on this website.
